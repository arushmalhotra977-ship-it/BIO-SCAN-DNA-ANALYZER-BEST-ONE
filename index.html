Scientific limitation: This is an educational simulation using text entered by visitors. It does not analyze biological samples, identify people, diagnose conditions, predict health outcomes or establish ancestry/relationships. Alignment scores depend on the scoring scheme; this demo uses match +2, mismatch −1 and gap −2.
